Starter notice · review before launch

1. Information this website collects

This static demo storefront does not create customer accounts, operate a payment checkout, or submit the contact form to a website database. The shopping cart is kept in the current browser page session and is not sent to a store server.

2. WhatsApp orders & messages

When you choose to check out or send a contact message, the website prepares text and opens WhatsApp. The message is not sent automatically; you review and send it. Once you use WhatsApp, your communications are handled under WhatsApp/Meta's terms and privacy practices. The business may retain information you send in order to respond to you or manage an order.

3. Information you choose to share

Order enquiries may include your name, phone number, delivery details, product choices, customisation requests, and conversation history. Share only details needed to handle your request, and avoid sending payment passwords, one-time passwords, or other secrets.

4. External links

This website links to services such as Instagram and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices. This storefront does not control how an external service processes information.

5. Cookies, analytics & payments

The current code does not intentionally use analytics tools, advertising pixels, a customer database, or an on-site payment processor. Hosting providers and third-party services may process technical information according to their own terms. If these tools are added later, this notice should be updated.

6. Contact & updates

For privacy questions, contact the business via the Contact Us page. The shop owner should add an effective date, contact channel, retention approach, and any other applicable details before publishing this notice as a final policy.
