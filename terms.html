A few ground rules

The little print.

These draft terms describe how this catalogue is intended to work. An order is not confirmed merely by adding items to the cart.

1. Catalogue & availability

This website is a product catalogue for Amanat by Anchal. Product names, demo descriptions, photographs or illustrations, and prices should be checked by the shop owner before launch. Availability may vary, and a product listing is not a guarantee that an item is in stock.

2. Placing an order

Using the WhatsApp checkout feature prepares an order message for you to review and send. The order becomes confirmed only when the business replies and confirms availability, customisation details, final price, delivery arrangements, and payment instructions.

3. Prices & payment

Prices are displayed in Indian rupees (INR). The business should confirm whether shipping, customisation, taxes or other charges apply and confirm the final total before payment. Do not treat a draft WhatsApp message as a receipt or proof of payment.

4. Handmade and personalised items

Handmade products can have minor differences from one item to another. Personalisation requests should be checked carefully before the order is confirmed. Any design, colour, spelling, or delivery deadline should be agreed with the business in writing.

5. Shipping, cancellations & returns

Shipping timelines, cancellation options, return/exchange rules, and remedies for damaged or incorrect orders must be confirmed with the business. Please read the Shipping & Returns page and ask questions before payment.

6. Website use & updates

Please use the website lawfully and provide accurate details when making an enquiry. The owner may update the catalogue or website content as products, prices, and business practices change. Any mandatory rights available under applicable law remain unaffected by this draft notice.
